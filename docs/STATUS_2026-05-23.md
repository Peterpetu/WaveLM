# WaveLM — Version Status

**Version:** v0.4.0 (muP width-scaled lr · capacity tooling)
**Date:** 2026-05-23
**One line:** A from-scratch complex wave-field **byte-level** LM that escaped the
predecessor's bigram ceiling, scales to 7.35M params on a 16 GB GPU, and learns
real diverse text (the Pile) from a cold start.

---

## 1. What WaveLM is (current as-built)

Byte-level (V=256, no tokenizer). State = complex field `ψ ∈ ℂ^{T×d}`. Per
internal step τ (n_sub of them): **dilated causal conv** over positions →
**low-rank mode coupling** (channel mixing, every `mix_every` steps) →
**per-channel saturating nonlinearity** `p/(1+g|p|²)` → **phase rotation** `e^{iω}`
→ **energy normalisation** `‖ψ[n]‖=√d`. Readout = **Born rule**
`|⟨W_E[o],ψ⟩|²/T_o` → softmax → next-byte CE. Optimiser AdamW (decay/no-decay
groups), cosine LR, grad-clip 1.0, complex64, activation checkpointing. Full math
in `00_master_plan.md` + `02_dynamics_and_gradients.md`; deviations in
`04_as_built.md`.

The one mechanism inherited from the predecessor (AIFS): the bilinear contrastive
embedding readout (now the Born rule). Everything building the contextual state —
complex carriers, interference, dilated propagation, energy normalisation, mode
coupling — is new.

## 3. Internal-metrics & run-behaviour analysis

**(a) Two-phase descent (universal).** Steep early drop = learning the byte-
frequency histogram (unigram); slow grind after = composition. On the Pile:
9.84→3.29 bpb by step 1000 (easy phase), then 3.29→2.30 over 11000 steps (the
hard phase). The early slope tells you nothing about data difficulty — it happens
on any dataset.

**(b) Gradient is clipped every step.** Grad-norm range 3–34 (mean ~7.4) vs
clip=1.0 → the update is scaled down 3–34× *every* step. No vanishing (energy
normalisation works), no explosion (bounded field). **Hypothesis at the time:**
clip=1.0 throttles the effective lr. **v0.4 update — TESTED and REJECTED:** under
Adam a per-step clip ~cancels (§1.2); the ablation found c∈{1,8,20,adaptive} within
0.009 bpb, *tightest clip marginally best*. Clipping is second-order; relaxing it is
not a convergence lever.

**(c) Carrier frequencies are frozen & self-stabilising.** `Var(ω)` stayed at
3.2899→3.2898 on the Pile (no drift at all; on TinyStories it drifted only −1.1%).
The conditional spectral-diversity guard **never activated** (`λ_ω=0` throughout).
The interference mechanism is rock-stable — the model keeps a maximally-spread
fixed carrier spectrum and learns everything else (embedding, kernel, mixing)
around it. The anti-collapse failure mode we built a safety net for does not
occur. (ω params barely train — uniform spectral init appears near-optimal.)

**(d) Generalises (no overfitting/leakage).** Held-out val tracked train the whole
way (val 2.30 ≈ train ~2.25 at step 12000); the model saw <60% of the 508 MB train
once, so memorisation is impossible. Monotone, smooth val curve.

**(e) Undertrained, not capacity-capped.** The Pile val was still descending at
step 12000 (2.373→2.245 over the last 380 steps). The biggest lever now is **more
steps + more data**, not more params.

**(f) Latency-bound, not compute-bound.** ~9k tok/s, 10.8 GB at d=2048; adding 4×
params (vs run3) barely changed throughput. Capacity is cheap; the per-step Python
loop over n_sub dominates time, so kernel fusion could 2–5× the speed.

**(g) bpb tracks coherence.** Samples climbed byte-soup (bpb 5) → letter-fragments
→ register-appropriate words+syntax (bpb 2.3). Long-range/sentence coherence needs
bpb < ~2, not yet reached.

## 4. Validated / working

- All 20 unit tests green (per-parameter FP64 finite-difference gradient checks;
  modular-addition composition gate proving escape from the bigram ceiling;
  streaming==parallel bit-exact).
- End-to-end: train, stream-generate, eval, byte-data pipeline, resume, warm-start.
- Scales 1M→7.35M on 16 GB; stable with lr scaled down for size.
- Learns both toy (TinyStories) and hard diverse (Pile) data from cold start.

## 5. Known limitations

- Not at big-model SOTA (Pile ~2.3 bpb vs ~1.0–1.2 for large tuned models); small
  + undertrained.
- Long-range coherence weak (sentence-level word-salad at 2.3 bpb).
- Throughput latency-bound at small d (kernel fusion evaluated and dropped: ~1.3×
  in the compute-bound training regime, no learning benefit — `07` §8).
- (Resolved in v0.4: lr now transfers across width via muP `--mup`; the grad-clip
  "throttle" was tested and is second-order, not a real limitation.)
- **Structural ceiling identified (doc 12, driver-attribution ablations).** The
  dominant drivers are *generic*: the per-channel saturation nonlinearity (off → +0.74
  bpb), channel mixing (off → +0.49), the contrastive embedding, and depth. The
  *wave-specific* parts are weak-to-decorative: the Born `|·|²` readout is **not**
  load-bearing — removing it *improves* bpb by 0.03–0.04 — and the carrier `ω` is minor
  (+0.008–0.019, shrinking with scale). Complex representation helps modestly (+0.20 at
  matched DOF, via the complex conv/mixing not the carrier). The real limit is the
  **absence of data-dependent routing** (WaveLM is ≈ a linear-time-invariant medium),
  which is exactly the architecture class with doc 11's shallow per-param scaling.

## 6. v0.4 changes and the resulting next levers

**v0.4 (2026-05-23) addressed the prior "next levers" — measured outcomes
(see `07_optimization_scaling_plan.md` §8):**
- **Relax grad-clip → MEASURED NEGATIVE.** Ablation (d=256, 600 steps, fixed seed):
  c∈{1,8,20,adaptive} span only 2.3277→2.3365 bpb, *monotone with the tightest
  (c=1) best*. Confirms §1.2: clipping is ~second-order under Adam. The adaptive-clip
  machinery enabled no new capability and improved nothing, so it was **reverted**
  (clip unchanged at `c=1`; one proof-test kept). The "clip throttles learning"
  hypothesis (item (b) below) is **rejected**.
- **muP width-scaled lr → SHIPPED.** Per-group lr ∝ 1/fan_in; the optimal base lr
  (3e-4) is now width-invariant (verified: forward-scale-invariant <2×, update-
  scale-invariant <3× across d=256→2048; d=512 smoke stable at 3e-4 where the
  un-scaled lr previously diverged). Enables capacity scaling without per-size lr
  search. Opt-in via `--mup`.
- **Kernel fusion → DROPPED.** ~1.3× in the compute-bound regime, no learning
  benefit, complex64 Triton unproven on Windows.
- **Capacity tooling → READY.** `tools/budget.py` rewritten model-exact (low-rank,
  ceiling P_pts); `untie_steps` (per-step kernel) added; `scripts/scaling_study.py`
  fits bpb≈E∞+A·N^−α. The ladder run itself is deferred (the one expensive gate).

**Remaining highest-value levers (evidence-ranked by the doc-12 ablations):**
1. **Data-dependent / selective dynamics** — the *structural* lever. WaveLM lacks the
   input-conditioned routing that powers attention/Mamba; a minimal selective gate
   already beats its parameter-matched control (−0.01 → −0.02 bpb, *growing* with
   scale). Richer input-steered conv / mixing / carriers is the route toward
   attention-class per-parameter scaling. (`--selective`, `selective_rank`.)
2. **`--untie_steps`** (per-step conv kernel) — best confirmed single win (−0.08 bpb at
   +0.4 % params); the tied propagation operator under-uses free capacity.
3. **Drop the Born `|·|²` readout** (linear readout) — free −0.03 → −0.04 bpb on real
   text (keep it only where a composition-gate nonlinearity is required).
4. **More steps + more data on the Pile** (curve still falling at 12k steps; ~295M of
   available bytes seen). With muP the scaling-law ladder runs without lr re-tuning.

## 7. Reproduce

```
# TinyStories (mode coupling):
python scripts/train_wave.py --steps 6000 --batch 96 --d 384 --n_sub 16 \
  --mix_channels --mix_rank 64 --mix_every 2 --lr 3e-4 --ckpt_dir checkpoints/x
# Pile bytes (build once), then train:
python scripts/prepare_pile_bytes.py
python scripts/train_wave.py --train_txt data/pile/train.bytes --val_txt data/pile/val.bytes \
  --steps 12000 --batch 24 --d 2048 --n_sub 16 --mix_channels --mix_rank 96 --mix_every 2 \
  --lr 1e-4 --seed 42 --ckpt_dir checkpoints/wavelm_pile_42
# eval / sample:
python scripts/eval_wave.py --ckpt <best.pt> --val_txt data/pile/val.bytes
python scripts/generate_wave.py --ckpt <best.pt> --max_new 200 --temperature 0.8 --top_k 40
```
